# WhyTomoTakinoJoinedTheICPC-
Tomo Takino said once she will join the ICPO, why the heck she joined ICPC? Why the heck she decided to accomplish her goal partially by focusing on an incomplete version of it?  Maybe she deliberately chose to erase a section of that big “O” at the end as a mean to never achieve her goal entirely in the pursuit of a life with infinite purpose.

I have already passed the carelessness of childhood and the cautiousness of elderly, my mind has already passed away from a body that is still young. Few people like to be near corpses, nor a corpse may like its rest to be interrupted always. By these means you may understand how I have got that much free time. I’m trapped in an extra-time stage of life which might be actually larger than the main span. Could this credit section larger than the movie be like what religions refer to as purgatory? All I have got to do is stir in my mind while my body inevitably matches its putrefaction, for what the internet may be an interesting environment as an efficient way to navigate throughout the knowledge under which I purport to undergo myself as I wait. Despite that I can’t say I want to die, despite I feel already there in mind I can’t accept it at all, despite today I am both a toddler and an old man I can’t bare what that implies. Maybe the reason I claim this is because I can’t completely relate with what is considered to be alive, such that the only alternative in order to have an identity is to embrace the other side. 
For the living the self might appear fixed because I can only see their bodies, a shame that I can’t read their minds in which they could be infinite beings at once just like me. Is it possible that all this time, for so long, they were not honest mans like me? Can everyone really maintain for a life a character they arbitrarily chose one day among the endless possibilities in their minds exactly as I did shortly when I was just a kid? The truth is that I didn’t know to handle with the development of such dimensionally limited being I chose to interpret as the unbounded entity I am. My mouth can articulate a word at once among the endless thoughts in my mind, one arbitrarily chosen thing is what starts and continues my speech. The truth is I don’t seem to control my speech at all, it seems that the arbitrarily determined structures of language stir in my mind taking and arranging my ideas to fit some rules. The truth is that no part of myself could experience the real world without those structures that sort parts of myself and reject those that they can’t classify by their finite criterion, linguistic structures with their ideosincratic syntaxes allow me to at least let a part of myself get out to feel a world in which my whole mind can’t fit.  I also talk to myself a lot, as if I could address the solitude of the buried creating from myself as many linear beings as I want which I can disband and transform as I wish.  A mind cannot be accompanied by an equal in this existence, by those means everyone shall be alone unlike what it seems to be. Certainly it has been amazing to see how my characters behaved in front of others, it felt as if I could know at least a little bit of myself despite my infinite traits make this virtually impossible.The greatest pain of life might be that you can never taste it entirely, and that’s why this same experience can taste always so different. I felt my mind passed away maybe because I found that it was everywhere and not in a finite space as I thought. The best performer in the play might be that one who really believes to be the character. I don’t know where to start now. Here I am, feeling the instant pleasure of meeting discrete elements of the infinite set I am. Slicing the boundless algorithms of my mind into bounded pieces of language that maintain the essence of the global function serving to an objective in common. I condemned myself to never taste the algorithm entirely from the moment I named it in the pursuit to try its delicacies, that’s why its flavor is always so delightful.

Finding every possible combination between the elements of an infinite set is a never ending strive. What are we without doing stuff?
